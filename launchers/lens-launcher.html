01

Who we are

Lentille Launcher (package com.cobr.lenslauncher) is an Android home screen that shows your apps as round icons in a honeycomb. It is developed by CoBru, an independent developer. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean CoBru.

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), CoBru is the data controller for the limited data described below.

02

In short

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account.
  • Your app list, home screen layout, searches and settings stay on your phone.
  • When the app crashes or hits an error, a technical report is sent to our crash reporting service, Sentry, so we can fix it. It does not identify you.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data for advertising.
03

Data kept on your device

To work as a launcher, the app keeps a few things in its own private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them, and we never receive them.

What Why
Your installed apps Read from Android to draw the honeycomb, search and launch apps, and show their shortcuts. The list itself is not saved.
Home screen setup Hidden apps, pinned shortcuts and how your Private Space is shown.
Preferences Your web search engine, "open single match" and similar settings.
Search text Kept only in memory while you type, to filter your apps. It is never saved or sent.
04

Crash reports

Lentille Launcher uses Sentry, a crash reporting service by Functional Software, Inc., to tell us when the app crashes, freezes or hits an unexpected error. This is the only reason the app uses the Internet.

What a report contains

  • The technical details of the error: error type and message, and where in the code it happened.
  • Device and app information: phone manufacturer and model, Android version, screen size, language, available memory and storage, app version.
  • A short trail of technical events before the error, such as which of the app's controls was tapped. It never includes what you type.
  • A random identifier created when the app is installed, which lets us tell whether many errors come from one phone or many. It is not linked to your name, your Google account or your phone's advertising ID.
  • App session information (when a session starts and ends, and whether it crashed), used to measure how stable each version is.
Reports do not include your name, email, contacts, location, photos, your list of apps or what you search for. An error message may occasionally mention the technical name of an app involved in the error (for example, an app that failed to open).

Where and how long

Reports are sent over an encrypted connection to Sentry's data centre in the European Union (Germany). Your IP address is used to deliver the report but is not stored with it. Reports are deleted automatically after at most 90 days.

Why

We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and fixing bugs (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). The data is kept to the minimum needed for that and is never used for anything else. You can object at any time by contacting us; uninstalling the app also stops all reports.

Permissions

Each permission below is used only for the feature described.

Permission Used for

Request app removal
REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES		 Opening Android's uninstall dialog when you choose Uninstall on an app. Android always asks you to confirm.

ACCESS_HIDDEN_PROFILES		 Showing, locking and unlocking your Private Space apps on Android 15 and later.

Lentille Launcher does not use usage access, accessibility services, location, contacts, the camera or the microphone.

06

Web search and other apps

Some actions hand you over to another app. When that happens, that app's own privacy policy applies, not this one.

  • Web search: when you search the web from Lentille Launcher, your query is opened in your browser with the engine you picked in settings (Google, Bing, Yandex, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia or Qwant), or handed to your phone's default search app. Lentille Launcher does not keep the query.
  • Wallpaper: opens Android's wallpaper picker. Lentille Launcher never reads or uploads your pictures.
  • App info, uninstall and system settings: open the matching Android screens.
07

Backups

Lentille Launcher allows Android's built-in backup. If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may save the app's settings to your Google account and restore them on a new phone. This is done by Google under your control and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy; we never have access to it.

08

Deleting your data

To erase everything stored on your phone, open Settings → Apps → Lentille Launcher → Storage and tap Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Crash reports are deleted automatically within 90 days; to have them deleted sooner, email us. Because reports don't identify you, we may ask for your app version and the approximate date of the crash to find them.

09

Your rights

Under the GDPR and other privacy laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete or move personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, write to us and we will answer within one month.

If you think your data has been mishandled, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live.

10

Lentille Launcher is a general-purpose app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; crash reports contain no information that identifies the person using the phone.
