01

Who we are

Oh Hi Launcher (package com.cobr.ohhilauncher) is a minimal, text-only home screen for Android, developed by CoBru, an independent developer. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean CoBru.

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), CoBru is the data controller for the limited data described below.

02

In short

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account.
  • Your app list, home screen, searches and settings stay on your phone.
  • When the app crashes or hits an error, a technical report is sent to our crash reporting service, Sentry, so we can fix it. It does not identify you.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data for advertising.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete the conversation once it is resolved, unless we need to keep it to meet a legal obligation.

03

Data kept on your device

To work as a launcher, the app stores a few things locally in its own private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them, and we never receive them. They leave your device only through Android's own backup (see Backups).

What Why
Your installed apps Read from Android to list, search and launch them. The list itself is not saved.
Home screen setup The apps you add to the home screen, renamed and hidden apps, the apps your swipes open, the clock and calendar apps you chose, and pinned shortcuts.
Preferences Theme, text size, bold font, alignment, date and time, status bar and similar settings.
App state Whether the welcome message and the first-run tips have been seen, and similar flags.
Search text Kept only in memory while you type, to filter your apps. It is never saved or sent.
04

Crash reports

Oh Hi Launcher uses Sentry, a crash reporting service by Functional Software, Inc., to tell us when the app crashes, freezes or hits an unexpected error. This is the only reason the app uses the Internet.

What a report contains

  • The technical details of the error: error type and message, and where in the code it happened.
  • Device and app information: phone manufacturer and model, Android version, screen size, language, available memory and storage, app version.
  • A short trail of technical events before the error, such as which of the app's controls was tapped. It never includes what you type.
  • A random identifier created when the app is installed, which lets us tell whether many errors come from one phone or many. It is not linked to your name, your Google account or your phone's advertising ID.
  • App session information (when a session starts and ends, and whether it crashed), used to measure how stable each version is.
Reports do not include your name, email, contacts, location, photos, your list of apps or what you search for. An error message may occasionally mention the technical name of an app involved in the error (for example, an app that failed to open).

Where and how long

Reports are sent over an encrypted connection to Sentry's data centre in the European Union (Germany). Your IP address is used to deliver the report but is not stored with it. Reports are deleted automatically after at most 90 days.

Why

We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and fixing bugs (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). The data is kept to the minimum needed for that and is never used for anything else. You can object at any time by contacting us; uninstalling the app also stops all reports.

06

Other apps and services

Some actions hand you over to another app. When that happens, that app's own privacy policy applies, not this one.

  • Web search: when a search in the app list matches no app, you can search the web; your query is handed to your phone's default search app. Oh Hi Launcher does not keep the query.
  • Wallpaper: opens your phone's wallpaper picker. Oh Hi Launcher never reads or uploads your pictures.
  • App info, uninstall and system settings: open the matching Android screens.
  • Other apps: the clock, the date and your swipes open the apps you chose, or your clock and calendar apps.
07

Backups

Oh Hi Launcher allows Android's built-in backup. If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may save the app's settings (home screen setup and preferences) to your Google account and restore them on a new phone. This is done by Google under your control and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy; we never have access to it.

You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

08

Deleting your data

To erase everything stored on your phone, open Settings → Apps → Oh Hi Launcher → Storage and tap Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Settings stored in an Android backup are managed from your Google account. Crash reports are deleted automatically within 90 days; to have them deleted sooner, email us. Because reports don't identify you, we may ask for your app version and the approximate date of the crash to find them.

09

Your rights

Under the GDPR and other privacy laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete or move personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, write to us and we will answer within one month.

If you think your data has been mishandled, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live.

10

Children

Oh Hi Launcher is a general-purpose app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; crash reports contain no information that identifies the person using the phone.
