01

Who we are

Oh Hi Launcher (package com.cobr.ohhilauncher) is a minimal, text-only home screen for Android, developed by CoBru, an independent developer based in Marseille, France. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean CoBru.

Oh Hi Launcher is a fork of Olauncher by tanujnotes and is distributed under the GNU GPLv3 licence. This policy covers Oh Hi Launcher only; it does not cover the original Olauncher app.

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), CoBru is responsible for this app.

02

What we collect

Nothing. Oh Hi Launcher does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send any data anywhere. It contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking SDKs and no account system.

We never receive your app list, your settings, your screen time or any other information about you or your device. The only way we learn anything about you is if you choose to write to us (see Contact).

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete the conversation once it is resolved, unless we need to keep it to meet a legal obligation.

03

Data kept on your device

To work as a launcher, the app stores a few things locally in its own private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them, and they never leave your device except through Android's own backup (see Backups).

What Why
Your installed apps Read from Android each time the drawer opens, to list and launch them. The list itself is not saved.
Home screen setup The apps you place on the home screen, renamed and hidden apps, gesture shortcuts and pinned shortcuts.
Preferences Theme, text size, alignment, clock and date options and similar settings.
Screen time Only if you turn it on: today's total is computed on the device from Android's usage statistics and shown on your home screen. It is not stored or shared.
App state Small counters such as when the app was first opened, used to decide when to show a tip or a one-time prompt.
04

Permissions

Each permission below is used only for the feature described, and only on your device.

Permission Used for
See all installed apps
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES		 Listing your apps in the drawer and on the home screen, which is the core job of a launcher.
Usage access
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS		 The optional screen time counter. You grant it yourself in Android settings and can revoke it at any time.
Request app removal
REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES		 Opening Android's uninstall dialog when you choose to uninstall an app from the drawer. Android always asks you to confirm.
Private Space
ACCESS_HIDDEN_PROFILES		 Showing, locking and unlocking your Private Space apps on Android 15 and later.
Expand status bar
EXPAND_STATUS_BAR		 Pulling down the notification shade when you swipe down on the home screen.
Set alarm
SET_ALARM		 Opening your clock app when you tap the time. The app never creates or changes alarms.
05

Accessibility service

Oh Hi Launcher includes an optional accessibility service that powers double tap to lock. It is off by default and only runs if you enable it yourself in Android's accessibility settings.

  • It listens only to taps inside Oh Hi Launcher itself; it is restricted to the app's own package and cannot see other apps.
  • Its only action is to lock the screen when you double tap an empty area of the home screen.
  • It does not read, collect, store or share anything you type or see on screen.

You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Accessibility on your phone; double tap to lock then stops working and nothing else changes.

06

Other apps and services

Some actions hand you over to another app. When that happens, that app's own privacy policy applies, not this one.

  • Web search: when you search the web from the drawer, your query is opened in your browser on DuckDuckGo. Oh Hi Launcher does not keep the query.
  • Rate and share: these open the Google Play Store or Android's share sheet.
  • System apps: gestures and shortcuts can open your clock, calendar, phone, camera or Android settings.

We have no partners and we do not sell, rent or share any data, because we do not have any.

07

Backups

Oh Hi Launcher allows Android's built-in backup. If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may save the app's settings (home screen setup and preferences) to your Google account and restore them on a new phone. This is done by Google under your control and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy; we never have access to it.

You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

08

Deleting your data

All the app's data lives on your phone. To erase it, open Settings → Apps → Oh Hi Launcher → Storage and tap Clear storage, or simply uninstall the app. Settings stored in an Android backup are managed from your Google account.

09

Your rights

Under the GDPR and other privacy laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete or move personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Since we hold no personal data from the app, these rights mostly concern emails you send us; write to us and we will answer within one month.

If you think your data has been mishandled, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or with the authority in your own country.

10
