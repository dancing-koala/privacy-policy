01

Who We Are

ZenFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is a guided breathing application available on Android. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal data when you download, install, or use the ZenFlow app.

By using ZenFlow, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the application.

02

Data We Collect

We follow a minimal data collection principle. We only collect what is strictly necessary to provide the service.

Category Data Purpose
Account Email address (if you create an account) Authentication, account recovery
Usage Exercises completed, session duration, streak data Progress tracking, personalization
Device OS version, device model, app version Bug diagnosis, compatibility
Crash logs Stack traces, error context (anonymized) App stability
We do not collect health data, microphone recordings, location, or contacts. Breathing exercises are processed entirely on-device.
03

How We Use Your Data

We use collected data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Providing and improving the ZenFlow application
  • Saving your progress and preferences across sessions
  • Diagnosing and fixing technical issues
  • Sending transactional emails (account confirmation, subscription receipts) — never marketing without explicit consent

We do not build advertising profiles, sell data, or use your information for any purpose not listed above.

04

Payments & Subscriptions

ZenFlow offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions processed exclusively through the Google Play Billing system.

We never see, store, or process your payment card details. All financial transactions are handled directly by Google. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how payment data is handled.

Regarding subscriptions:

  • Subscriptions auto-renew at the end of each billing period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time via Google Play → Subscriptions.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period. No partial refunds are issued except where required by applicable law.
  • Prices may change; you will be notified in advance of any price increases.

For refund requests, please contact Google Play support directly, as all billing is managed by Google.

05

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share limited data with the following categories of third parties, strictly for operational purposes:

  • Crash reporting (e.g. Firebase Crashlytics): anonymized error logs only
  • Analytics (if applicable): aggregated, non-identifiable usage statistics
  • Legal obligation: if required by law or valid legal process

Any third-party service we use is bound by data processing agreements that prohibit them from using your data for their own purposes.

06

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Your personal data is deleted within 30 days.
  • Anonymized, aggregated data (not linked to you) may be retained for analytical purposes.
  • Backup copies are purged within 90 days.

You can request account deletion at any time from within the app or by contacting us.

07

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: object to certain types of processing
  • Withdraw consent: at any time, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in the EU/EEA and believe we are processing your data unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

08

Children's Privacy

ZenFlow is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will delete it promptly.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

09

Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Access controls limiting who can access personal data internally
  • Regular security reviews of our infrastructure

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take all reasonable precautions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify you via the app or email.

Continued use of ZenFlow after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
