Breathe.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how the Breathe. application (package ID: com.cobr.optimhale), developed by Corentin Bruneau, handles your information. We take your privacy seriously — and in this case, that means keeping things very simple.

01 Information We Collect

Breathe. does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The application runs entirely on your device. No account creation is required, and no data is sent to external servers.

All breathing session data, progress, and preferences remain on your device at all times.

02 Data Shared With Third Parties

We do not share any data with third parties. Breathe. does not integrate advertising networks, analytics services, or any external data processors.

As declared on the Google Play Store, no data is shared with third parties.

03 Permissions

Breathe. may request access to the following device capabilities:

  • Vibration — used for haptic cues that guide your breathing rhythm. No data is collected through this feature.
  • Keep screen on — to prevent the screen from turning off during a session. No data is collected through this feature.

No permission is used to collect, track, or transmit information about you.

04 Local Storage

Breathe. may store your session preferences and progress locally on your device (e.g., chosen breathing patterns, session history) to provide a consistent experience. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

You can remove this data at any time by uninstalling the application.

05 Children's Privacy

Breathe. is rated for ages 3 and up. Because the application does not collect any personal data, it is safe for use by children. No information is gathered from users of any age.

06 Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted by Breathe., there is no risk of your personal information being compromised through the use of this application. Your data stays where it belongs — on your device.

07 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the application after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
